Our Story

Africa-rooted.
Globally competitive.

We're a Nairobi-based digital marketing agency obsessed with one thing: measurable, compounding growth for the brands we work with.

Who We Are

Built for brands
that refuse to be ordinary

Founded in 2015, Avalor grew from a two-person consultancy into one of Nairobi's most trusted growth marketing agencies. We work with early-stage startups, Series A companies, and established Kenyan and East African brands that are serious about growth.

What makes us different? We're not a creative agency that dabbles in performance. We're a performance agency with world-class creative. Everything we do is tied to a number — and we report on it every week.

"Most agencies sell you deliverables. We sell you outcomes. Every channel, every creative, every ad dollar — engineered around one thing: your growth."
