TikTok Ads in Kenya:
what's working
right now

TikTok advertising in the Kenyan market has unique dynamics. We share the creative formats, targeting, and budget structures giving us the best results.

TikTok crossed 5 million active users in Kenya in early 2026 — and the ad ecosystem is still immature enough that early movers are getting extraordinary results before competition drives CPMs up. We've been running TikTok campaigns for Kenyan brands for two years now. Here's what we've learned.

The Kenyan TikTok audience

The Kenyan TikTok audience skews younger than Meta — 18–34 dominates — and is overwhelmingly mobile-first. Mobile data costs have dropped significantly with affordable bundles, which means video consumption is high even in Tier 2 cities like Eldoret, Kisumu, and Nakuru.

Culturally, content that feels Kenyan converts better than globally-produced creative. Sheng, local references, Kenyan music in the background, and familiar settings (matatu, CBD, local markets) outperform polished international-style ads almost every time.

"On TikTok Kenya, authenticity beats production value. A founder talking to camera in their warehouse outperforms a $2,000 studio shoot."

Creative formats that work

1. Problem-solution hook (3-second rule)

Open with the problem your customer has — out loud, on screen, immediately. "Tired of [problem]?" works because TikTok users scroll at speed. You have 1–3 seconds before they're gone. Lead with the pain, then introduce the solution. This format consistently gets 2–4× the hook rate of brand-led openings.

2. UGC-style reviews

Someone holding your product and talking naturally about it, on their phone, in their home. No fancy lighting. No script. The less produced, the more trusted. For e-commerce, UGC review ads with real customers outperform everything else by 30–60% in conversion rate.

3. "Did you know" educational content

Quick educational hooks work particularly well for fintech, health, and B2B brands where trust is required before purchase. "Did you know most Kenyans overpay for [category] by X%?" — then explain, then solve. Builds authority fast.

4. Before/after transformations

Works for beauty, fitness, home, and any product with visible results. The TikTok format (quick cut between before and after) is native to how people consume on the platform. Don't overthink it.

Targeting that actually converts

Broad targeting + creative quality

TikTok's algorithm is exceptional at finding buyers when given enough signal. We run most of our Kenyan campaigns on broad targeting with no interest stacking — and let the algorithm optimise. The creative quality is the targeting. A great creative finds its audience.

Retargeting: video viewers + website visitors

Build custom audiences from 50%+ video viewers and website visitors, then retarget with direct-response creative. These audiences are small in Kenya right now but they convert at 3–5× the rate of cold audiences.

Budget and scaling approach

  • Start with $500–$1,000/mo to find 1–2 winning creatives before scaling
  • Scale winning ad sets by 20–30% every 3–5 days — aggressive scaling resets the algorithm
  • Refresh creative every 3–4 weeks — TikTok audiences fatigue faster than Meta in smaller markets
  • Run 3–5 creative concepts simultaneously — on TikTok, creative testing IS your targeting strategy

The biggest mistakes we see

  • Running Meta-style creative on TikTok — static images, logo-forward, polished production. It doesn't work.
  • Starting with too small a budget ($100–$200/mo) — the algorithm can't optimise without data volume
  • Not installing the TikTok Pixel correctly — without server-side events, your targeting and optimisation is broken
  • Targeting too narrowly — interest stacking in a market the size of Kenya kills reach
  • Stopping campaigns that haven't had 50 conversion events — you can't judge performance before the algorithm has enough data
