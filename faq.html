General

Avalor is a full-service digital marketing agency based in Nairobi, Kenya. We run the complete growth engine for startups and scaling brands — SEO, paid advertising (Meta, TikTok, Google), email and SMS marketing, content strategy, ROAS tracking, and conversion rate optimisation. Think of us as your outsourced growth team, not just a vendor.

Our sweet spot is startups from pre-seed through Series B — companies that have a product-market fit and are ready to accelerate growth but don't yet have a full in-house marketing team. We also work with established SMEs and corporates looking to modernise their digital marketing. We operate across Kenya, East Africa, and internationally.

Three things: (1) We're ROAS-first. Every decision ties back to return on investment, not vanity metrics. (2) We're a unified growth system — your SEO, ads, email, and CRO are all connected and feeding each other. (3) We operate on rolling 30-day terms, so we earn your business every single month. No hiding behind long contracts.

We're based at The Pinnacle, Westlands, Nairobi, Kenya. Our team works remotely across Kenya and East Africa, and we serve clients across the continent and internationally. You can reach us at avalormarketingagency@gmail.com or +254 141 510 631.

Services

We manage Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok Ads, and Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube). Meta is included from Growth plan upward. TikTok is included from Growth. Google is included in Scale and available as an add-on for Growth. All three are included in Enterprise.

Yes — organic social media management (content calendars, post creation, scheduling, and community engagement) is available as part of our services. It is separate from paid ads management. Ask about it during your strategy call and we'll build it into your proposal if it fits your goals.

Yes. Ad copywriting and creative direction are included in all packages. For static image and video creative production, this is included from Growth upward. If you have an in-house creative team, we can work with your assets — or we can fully own creative end-to-end.

We work with Klaviyo, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Brevo (formerly Sendinblue), and HubSpot. For SMS, we work with Twilio, Africa's Talking, and Klaviyo SMS. We can work within your existing platform or recommend the best fit for your business model.

We don't build websites from scratch, but we do landing page CRO — optimising existing pages to convert better. For full website builds, we can refer you to trusted partners and collaborate on the brief to make sure the site is built to convert from day one.

Pricing

Our retainer packages start at $1,500/mo (Starter), $3,000/mo (Growth), and $6,000/mo (Scale). Enterprise is custom-priced. These fees cover our team's strategy, creative, and management work. Ad spend is separate and goes directly from you to the platforms — we never mark it up. See the full pricing page for details.

Minimum ad spend mirrors your retainer tier: $1,500/mo on Starter, $3,000/mo on Growth, and $6,000/mo on Scale. This ensures there's enough budget to generate meaningful data and results. Ad spend goes directly to the ad platforms and is entirely separate from our fee.

No setup fees for standard packages. If your accounts need a full conversion tracking rebuild (GA4, pixels, GTM), that may be quoted as a one-time engagement ($500–$1,000) before the retainer begins. We'll flag this upfront during onboarding.

We invoice in USD by default. Kenyan clients may pay in KES at the prevailing CBK exchange rate on the invoice date. We also accept M-Pesa and bank transfer for local clients. International clients pay via bank transfer or Wise.

Process & Onboarding

After signing, you receive an onboarding form and access-sharing checklist. We schedule a kickoff call within 24 hours to align on goals, brand voice, and target audiences. Account setup and campaign builds take 48–72 hours. You'll have campaigns live before most agencies have finished their "discovery phase."

You'll have access to a live dashboard (Looker Studio or equivalent) showing real-time campaign performance. On Growth and above, you receive bi-weekly or weekly strategy calls. All plans receive a monthly performance report. If something underperforms, you hear about it from us first — we don't wait for you to notice.

Every account has a lead strategist who owns your growth plan and is your primary point of contact. Behind them is a team of channel specialists — an SEO lead, ads manager, email specialist, and analyst. On Scale and Enterprise, you also get a dedicated account strategist as a single-point-of-contact.

Always. We work inside your ad accounts, not ours. Your Meta Business Manager, Google Ads account, and all campaigns belong to you. If we part ways, you keep everything — your history, audiences, pixels, and creative assets. We never hold accounts hostage.

Results & Expectations

Across our client base we average 3.8× ROAS on paid campaigns, a 62% reduction in cost-per-acquisition, and 380%+ organic traffic growth over 12 months. These are averages — your results depend on your industry, offer quality, market size, and starting baseline. During your strategy call, we give you a realistic projection specific to your business, not a generic promise.

Paid ads typically show meaningful data within 2–4 weeks. Optimised ROAS usually stabilises by week 6–8 as the algorithm learns. SEO is a longer play — you'll see early movement in 60–90 days, with compounding results building over 6–12 months. Email marketing shows results from the first send. We set realistic timelines for each channel during onboarding.

We don't guarantee specific ROAS or ranking numbers — no honest agency should, because results depend on market conditions, platform algorithms, and factors outside any agency's control. What we guarantee: best-practice execution, full transparency, weekly reporting, and continuous optimisation. We'll always tell you the truth about what's working and what isn't.

Contracts & Cancellation

No. All retainers are rolling 30-day terms from day one. We ask for 30 days' written notice to cancel — purely for an orderly handover of accounts and deliverables. There are no 6- or 12-month lock-ins, no early termination fees.

Email us at avalormarketingagency@gmail.com with 30 days' notice before your next billing date. We'll wrap up all active deliverables, export and hand over all data, assets, and account accesses, and part on good terms. We want to be the kind of agency you'd recommend even if you leave.

Your campaigns, audiences, pixel data, and all account history belong to you and remain in your accounts. We remove our team members' access, prepare a full handover doc, and you're free to manage everything yourself or bring in another agency. Nothing is deleted. Nothing is taken.
